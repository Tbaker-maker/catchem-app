2026-08-23 AFFECTED A PUBLISHED NUMBER
Our marketplace gap was not comparing like with like. The eBay figure included shipping; TCGplayer market prices do not. On cheap items — packs especially — this reversed the answer: we showed some packs asking far more on eBay when, once shipping was counted on both sides, they were actually cheaper there.
Fixed: Both sides now include shipping and neither includes tax, which is stated wherever a price appears. Journey Together booster packs moved from +54.9% to -12.7%; Destined Rivals from +7.5% to -30%. Sealed boxes barely changed, because both marketplaces ship those free.
So it can't recur: The comparison basis is recorded on every row, and the shipping estimate is labelled as an estimate with a stated condition for re-checking it.

2026-08-21 CAUGHT BEFORE PUBLICATION
Share cards were minted in a format social platforms reject, and long text inside them was being dropped when converted.
Fixed: Cards now render as PNG with the text drawn natively.
So it can't recur: Every card rasterizes as part of the daily run, so the format that ships is the format that was checked.
2026-08-21 AFFECTED A PUBLISHED NUMBER
Our Pokemon GO Elite Trainer Box figure read high. Multi-quantity listings ("lot of 2", "2x", sealed cases) were passing our filters and lifting medians on any product with an active lot market.
Fixed: The product is held out of published reads until it re-verifies. The filter now rejects multi-item listings across all 207 products.
So it can't recur: A multi-item pattern guard runs at fetch time, and a durable quarantine file keeps a held product held even through a full data rebuild.
2026-08-20 CAUGHT BEFORE PUBLICATION
Our sealed index baseline included the day we changed pricing methods, so part of the first move measured our own cleanup rather than the market.
Fixed: The baseline was moved to the first full day under the current method and the history rebuilt from source snapshots.
So it can't recur: New sets also sit out their first 90 days, so launch-window noise never enters the index.
